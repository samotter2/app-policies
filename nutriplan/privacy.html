NutriPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

NutriPlan ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

NutriPlan AI collects the following information that you provide during onboarding and app usage:

  • Dietary preferences and restrictions (e.g., keto, vegan, gluten-free)
  • Ingredients you want to avoid (allergies and dislikes)
  • Health and nutrition goals (e.g., lose weight, gain muscle)
  • Cooking skill level and meal prep time preferences
  • Daily calorie and macronutrient targets
  • Meal tracking and daily food log entries
  • Messages sent to the AI nutritionist chat

How Your Data Is Stored

Your personal data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not maintain user accounts or a database containing your personal information. When you use AI-powered features, relevant information is securely transmitted to our backend and to OpenAI only for the purpose of generating meal plans, grocery lists, meal details, meal swaps, and chat responses.

Third-Party Services

NutriPlan uses OpenAI's API to generate personalized meal plans, grocery lists, and AI chat responses. When you generate a meal plan or use the AI chat feature, your dietary preferences, restrictions, and relevant profile information are sent to OpenAI's servers to produce a response. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS.

OpenAI's use of this data is governed by their own privacy policy and data usage policies. We encourage you to review OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com/privacy.

We do not sell your personal data. We use trusted service providers only when needed to operate NutriPlan AI, such as generating AI responses, hosting the app's backend, and processing App Store subscriptions.

Subscription and Payment Data

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information such as credit card numbers or billing addresses. All payment processing is managed by Apple in accordance with their privacy practices.

Analytics and Tracking

NutriPlan does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track your behavior, location, or device information for advertising purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since personal app data is stored locally on your device, you have control over it. Deleting the app from your device will remove locally stored data including your dietary profile, meal plans, grocery lists, tracking history, and chat messages. We do not maintain user accounts or a database containing your personal information.

Children's Privacy

NutriPlan is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
