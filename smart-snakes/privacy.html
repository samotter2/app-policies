Smart Snakes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Operator: Bugcraft Labs

About Smart Snakes

Smart Snakes is a puzzle game for a general audience. No player account is required. Core gameplay works offline; ads, purchases, purchase recovery and web pages need a connection and available services.

The app does not ask for a date of birth or verify age. The App Store rating describes content suitability.

Information on your device

The game stores progress, coins, lives, helpers, preferences and purchase records on your device to save progress, deliver rewards and prevent duplicate purchase grants. There is no cloud wallet or automatic cloud recovery. Deleting the app or changing devices may lose your coins, including unused purchased coins. Restore purchases does not recover coins from completed consumable purchases. Apple may redeliver an unfinished purchase for verification and completion once.

The game's diagnostic logs stay on your device unless you share them for support. They may include device model, app version, errors, timing, performance and game-state summaries. SDK processing is described below. Deleting the app normally removes local data; device or Apple backups may retain copies according to your settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

We use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and regional privacy messages. Our launch advertising policy is nonpersonalized, with publisher first-party ID disabled and a general-audience (G) content filter. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google and participating advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location derived from it, app- or device-scoped identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. They use this information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, service operation and improvement, and fraud prevention. Our configuration includes Google Advertising Products and other advertising and measurement partners from Google's configured lists. Regional messages identify the applicable partners and purposes in their partner options. Nonpersonalized ads still collect information and may involve partners beyond Google.

Where required, Google's privacy message appears before ads are requested. Use Settings → Privacy choices, where available, to review or change your regional choices. Supported US states offer an opt-out from the sale or sharing of personal information where available. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, choices include Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. Forms require internet access. Ads may be unavailable after refusal, errors, unsupported consent signals or service restrictions. Skipping rewarded ads does not prevent core puzzle play.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases and Unity purchasing telemetry

Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment-card details. Transaction verification and local purchase records let the app deliver coins and prevent duplicate grants. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Unity In-App Purchasing is configured to send purchase telemetry to Unity for reporting and analytics: product and transaction details, available receipts or signed transaction records, a vendor-scoped device identifier, and device, operating-system and installation information. This is separate from local saves and does not require a player account. Google's advertising privacy choices do not control Unity's purchase telemetry. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

Email bugcraftlabs+smartsnakes@gmail.com for support or to request access, correction or deletion of support information. We receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. In-app support opens your email app for you to review and send the message. Gmail provides our email service under Google's policies.

Bugcraft Labs uses support messages only to respond to requests, not for advertising, marketing, analytics, personalization or tracking. We delete them once the issue is resolved and they are no longer needed for support or legal obligations. Provider backups may retain copies. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may receive technical request information, such as your IP address. The pages contain no ads, analytics scripts, forms or third-party fonts. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Providers, retention and changes

Google, Apple and Unity retain information under their own policies. Contact them about records they independently control. See Google's retention explanation. We update this policy and its effective date when our practices change.
