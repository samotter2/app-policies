Smart Snakes Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Operator: Bugcraft Labs, an Ontario sole proprietorship

About Smart Snakes

Smart Snakes is a puzzle game. No player account is required. Core gameplay works offline; advertising, purchases, purchase recovery and web pages need network or service availability.

The game is intended for a general audience. The app does not ask for a date of birth or verify a player's age. The App Store age rating describes content suitability and does not establish an individual player's age.

Information on your device

The game stores progress, coins, lives, helper inventory, preferences and purchase records on your device to operate the game, save progress, deliver rewards and prevent duplicate purchase grants. The game does not offer an account-based cloud wallet or automatic cloud recovery. Deleting the app or changing devices may lose your remaining coins, including unused purchased coins. Restore purchases does not recreate an already fulfilled consumable coin balance. Apple may redeliver an unfinished purchase so the app can verify and complete it once.

The game's own diagnostic logs stay on your device unless you choose to share them for support. They can include device model, app version, errors, timing, performance and game-state summaries. This local-log statement does not cover the separate SDK processing described below. Deleting the app normally removes its local data; backups managed by your device or Apple may retain copies according to your settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and applicable privacy messages. Our launch advertising policy is nonpersonalized, with publisher first-party ID disabled. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or intentionally use the advertising identifier (IDFA). AdMob requests use a general-audience (G) ad content filter. Applicable privacy messages and service eligibility may make rewarded ads unavailable.

Nonpersonalized advertising still processes information. Google and any participating advertising partners may process your IP address and approximate location derived from it, app- or device-scoped identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. These services use information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, service operation and improvement, and fraud prevention. Our AdMob configuration includes Google Advertising Products and other advertising and measurement partners from Google's configured partner lists. The regional privacy messages identify the applicable partners and their purposes; you can review those lists in the message's partner options. Nonpersonalized ads do not mean that only Google processes information or that no information is collected.

Where required, the app presents Google's privacy message before requesting ads. Use Settings → Privacy choices when that option is available to review or change the choices supported for your region. Where available in supported US states, regional privacy options allow you to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the message offers Consent, Do not consent and Manage options. Privacy forms require internet access. Ads may be unavailable following refusal, an error or unsupported consent signals. Choosing to skip a rewarded ad does not prevent core puzzle play.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Purchases and Unity purchasing telemetry

Apple processes payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple transaction verification and local purchase records let the app deliver coins and prevent duplicate grants. Apple handles its information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Unity In-App Purchasing is configured to send purchase telemetry to Unity, including product and transaction details, available purchase receipts or signed transaction records, a vendor-scoped device identifier, and device, operating-system and installation information, for purchase reporting and analytics. This processing is separate from the game's local save and does not require a player account. Advertising privacy choices in Google's messages do not control this separate Unity purchasing telemetry. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Support and privacy requests

Email bugcraftlabs+smartsnakes@gmail.com for support or requests to access, correct or delete support information. We receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. The in-app support action opens your email application; you review and send the message yourself. Gmail provides our email service under Google's policies.

The operator handles the support inbox. We use support messages to respond to requests, not for advertising, marketing, analytics, personalization or tracking. We delete messages when the issue is resolved and they are no longer needed for support or legal obligations. Provider-managed backups may retain copies. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or unrelated personal information.

Website hosting

These privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them may provide GitHub with technical request information such as your IP address. The pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or third-party fonts. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Providers, retention and changes

Google, Apple and Unity retain information under their own policies. Use their privacy-request routes for records they independently control. See Google's retention explanation. We update this policy and its effective date when our practices change.
