Smart Snakes Support

For help, feedback or a problem, email bugcraftlabs+smartsnakes@gmail.com.

Playing

Grab a snake's face and drag through adjacent tiles. Tap and release to flip its direction, then grab the new face. Guide every snake to its matching colored home. Move order matters when snakes share tight spaces.

Daily unlocks after six different campaign first clears. Daily boards are untimed and do not cost lives. Campaign levels, including replays of completed levels, use the normal timed campaign rules. Pause lets you resume or restart; restarting resets the board and its starting time, but does not refund helpers already used. Sound and haptics can be changed in Settings.

Saved progress and coins

Progress, coins and preferences are stored on your device. Reopen the game and use Resume after an interruption. Deleting the app or changing devices may lose your remaining coins, including unused purchased coins. There is no automatic cloud recovery. Avoid deleting the app to troubleshoot a missing purchase or save.

Purchases and Restore

Shop offers a Small Coin Pack (500 coins) and a Medium Coin Pack (1600 coins). The app displays the current price supplied by Apple before purchase. Prices and availability depend on your storefront and service availability.

Restore purchases does not recreate an already fulfilled consumable coin balance. An unfinished purchase may be checked and completed once when the store reconnects. If a purchase seems interrupted, keep the app installed, reconnect and use Settings → Retry services. Contact support if the problem remains. Do not send your full receipt or payment details in an initial support message.

For a refund request, use Apple's Report a Problem service; Apple determines eligibility.

Ads and privacy

Smart Snakes is intended for a general audience. The app does not verify a player's age; the App Store rating describes content suitability.

Rewarded ads are optional. An ad may be unavailable because of privacy choices, regional treatment, internet connectivity or service availability. Closing an ad before earning its reward does not grant a reward. Core puzzle play works offline. Purchases, rewarded ads, service recovery and web pages need a network connection.

Where available, Settings → Privacy choices opens the regional advertising choices. Read the Privacy Policy for local storage, SDK processing and support information.

What to include in a support request

Tell us your app version/build, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, mode/level and what happened. A screenshot or short recording can help. Include the pack name and approximate time for a purchase issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information. We cannot promise recovery of a confirmed coin balance lost after deleting the app or changing devices.
