Playing

Drag a snake's face through adjacent tiles. Tap and release to flip its direction, then drag the new face. Guide every snake to its matching colored home. Move order matters in tight spaces.

Daily unlocks after first clearing six different campaign levels. Daily boards are untimed and cost no lives. Campaign levels and replays are timed. Pause to resume or restart; restarting resets the board and timer but does not refund used helpers. Change sound and haptics in Settings.

Saved progress and coins

Progress, coins and preferences stay on your device. After an interruption, reopen the game and use Resume. Deleting the app or changing devices may lose your coins, including unused purchased coins; there is no automatic cloud recovery. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting a purchase or save.

Purchases and Restore

The Shop offers Small (500 coins) and Medium (1600 coins) Coin Packs. Apple's current price appears before purchase. Prices and availability depend on your storefront and available services.

Restore purchases does not recover coins from completed consumable purchases. An unfinished purchase may be verified and completed once when the store reconnects. For an interrupted purchase, keep the app installed, reconnect and use Settings → Retry services. Contact support if the problem continues. Do not send a full receipt or payment details in your initial message.

Request refunds through Apple's Report a Problem service; Apple determines eligibility.

Ads and privacy

Smart Snakes is for a general audience. The app does not verify age; the App Store rating describes content suitability.

Rewarded ads are optional and may be unavailable because of privacy choices, regional rules, connectivity or service availability. Closing an ad before earning its reward grants no reward. Core puzzles work offline; purchases, ads, service recovery and web pages need a connection.

Where available, Settings → Privacy choices opens regional advertising choices. The Privacy Policy explains local storage, SDK data processing and support information.

What to include in a support request

Include your app version/build, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, mode/level and what happened. Screenshots or a short recording can help. For purchases, include the pack name and approximate time. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information.
